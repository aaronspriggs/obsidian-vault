Common patterns

Panel, switch, panel

The switch sits between two panels. Its ports patch 1U up or 1U down with 6-inch cables, one panel port per switch port. This is the layout suggested most often for UniFi homelabs with 16 to 24 ports.

How to use a patch panel in a home lab rack · Patch cord length planning (6" for adjacent units)

Panel, cable manager, switch

The enterprise version puts a 1U horizontal cable manager between each panel and its switch. Runs are longer, and the extra rack unit holds the slack. It suits dense racks with 48-port switches more than a 16-port homelab.

Your rack today

Gateway, panel, switch. The panel is shared: the UDM Pro patches down to it and the Pro Max 16 patches up. That keeps runs short, but one 24-port panel serves two devices.
