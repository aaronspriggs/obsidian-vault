# Rack wall mount confirmed — 13/16" board screwed to studs

Confirmed rack mounting method: 12U RackPath wall mount is secured to a 13/16" plywood/MDF backing board, which is itself screwed into wall studs. This is a solid installation. Load is distributed across the full board rather than concentrated at two stud points. Real-world capacity well above rack's nominal 110 lb rating. Estimated total rack weight at full planned build: 133–138 lbs (not including rack itself). Stud-backed mount handles this safely.
