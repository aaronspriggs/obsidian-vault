## What changed

No system changes. The patch panel map for the 30U rack went from v4 to v12 in one session with Aaron on 2026-11-15, after the planning start on 2026-11-14, and he ordered a USW Enterprise 48 PoE on the way. This note holds the dated decision lines for each version and the artifact notes, moved here from the [project](<../projects/Re-terminate and label the patch panels for the 30U rack.md>) note the same day when it was slimmed under the rulebook's size rule; the text is as it stood in the note.

## Why

The project note keeps the agreed map (v12), its rules and the decisions that still stand. The steps that led there (which gear each version assumed, what each change fixed) explain why the map looks the way it does and are kept here.
